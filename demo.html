Power control for over‑the‑air federated learning under fading

Saurabh Suresh (24BCE2750) and Advaith Sivakumar (24BCE2715) · Team 24, Communication Networks, VIT SCOPE

All devices transmit at once and the channel adds their gradients. This lab compares how each power-control policy copes when some of those channels fade. Symbols have unit power, so the SNR is the per-device transmit SNR, and every number comes from the paper's simulation.

Setup

scikit-learn digits (1,347 train / 450 test), softmax regression, K = 50 devices, Dirichlet 0.5 label skew, channel realisations 400–499 for reporting. Symbols have unit power, so the SNR is the per-device transmit SNR; the server adds about 30 dB of coherent gain, which is why the interesting range is −45 to −25 dB. Accuracy is the mean of the last ten rounds over ten seeds.

Policies

OPT follows the threshold structure of Cao et al. (2020), C12 the Case I policy of Cao et al. (2022), Z10 Theorem 1 of Zhang and Tao (2021), and Y4 the device selection of Yang et al. (2020); these are our re-implementations, with tuned or replaced constants. Y14 is our own policy inspired by Yu et al. (2023). The suffix U means unit-gain normalisation: the server divides by the sum of the received amplitudes.
