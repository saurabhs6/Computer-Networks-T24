Power control for over‑the‑air federated learning under fading

All devices transmit at once and the channel adds their gradients. This lab compares how each power-control policy copes when some of those channels fade. Every number comes from the paper's simulation.

  • K 50 devices
  • h ~ CN(0, 1)
  • model softmax, d = 650
  • data digits, Dirichlet 0.5
  • runs 100 rounds × 10 seeds

Setup

scikit-learn digits (1,347 train / 450 test), softmax regression, K = 50 devices, channel-trace indices 400–499 for reporting. SNR is defined on normalised symbols, so −35 to −15 dB is the interesting range. Accuracy is the mean of the last ten rounds.

Controls and baselines

M2+EIG, OPT, RCI and C12 are added in Section 3.5 of the paper. OPT is our own implementation of the threshold structure of Cao et al. (2020). RCI is a generic regularised inversion. C12 re-implements the Case I policy of Cao et al. (2022) with tuned constants. Neither OPT nor C12 is the authors' code.
