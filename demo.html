Over-the-air federated learning under fading

Compare three power-control methods on the same simulated network: 50 devices, Rayleigh fading, non-IID digit data. Every number comes from the paper's simulation (10 seeds).

Saurabh Suresh · 24BCE2750
Advaith Sivakumar · 24BCE2715
Setup: scikit-learn digits (1,347 train / 450 test), softmax regression (650 parameters), Dirichlet 0.5 label skew, K = 50 devices, 100 rounds per run, ten seeds, channel trace indices 400–499 for reporting. SNR is defined on normalised symbols, so −35 to −15 dB is the interesting range. Accuracy is the mean of the last ten rounds. M2+EIG and OPT are controls added in Section 3.5 of the paper; OPT is our own implementation of the threshold structure of Cao et al. (2020) and is not their algorithm.
